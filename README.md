# Sampling barriers for real Gaussian phase retrieval

Yitzchak Shmalo, Einstein Institute of Mathematics, The Hebrew University of Jerusalem

- Paper: [T321_Phase_Retrieval_Sampling.pdf](T321_Phase_Retrieval_Sampling.pdf)
- LaTeX source: [T321_Phase_Retrieval_Sampling.tex](T321_Phase_Retrieval_Sampling.tex)

The Gibbs measure of the empirical quartic loss for noiseless real phase retrieval, with d >= 100 and m = 10^6 d Gaussian measurements. On one data event of probability at least 1 - 10 exp(-d/2), the measure concentrates near the two minimizers and has an exponentially large Poincare constant at inverse temperature of order d/epsilon, while a bounded convexification shows that the Poincare and log-Sobolev constants are finite. The paper proves a mixing lower bound for MALA from a 2-warm start and a finite all-start upper bound for a ball-truncated version. The obstruction is the separation of the two signs in raw parameter space.

Build with `pdflatex T321_Phase_Retrieval_Sampling.tex` (twice). The source carries its own bibliography and needs no data.
